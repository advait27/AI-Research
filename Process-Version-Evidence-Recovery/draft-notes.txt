The manuscript reconstructs the published HICSS specifications in Word; it does not claim to be an unmodified official template file. It uses US Letter, two columns, 10-point Times New Roman body text, 14-point title, 12/11-point headings, 9-point captions and references, no page numbers or headers/footers, a 140-word abstract, five keywords, and alphabetized author-date references. The PDF renderer embeds metric-compatible Liberation Serif/Sans substitutes; Word styles retain Times New Roman and Arial.

Scientific scope
The revised paper reports two completed simulation studies. Study I separates substantive benefit from declared prerequisite support. Study II evaluates separate process and observation versions, noisy diagnostics, imperfect repairs, eight policies, and three budgets. It contains 2,880 episodes per policy and 23,040 evaluation traces. The decision-loss policy spends less but recovers fewer supported beneficial claims than full revalidation. A deliberately faulty bookkeeping ablation exposes wrong-version endorsements under identical deployment outcomes. No LLM, real manufacturing deployment, or general first-ever novelty is established.
